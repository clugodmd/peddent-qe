Getting Started

What is PedBoards QE?

PedBoards QE is a dedicated question bank and study platform built specifically for pediatric dentistry board exam preparation. It provides high-yield, exam-style questions covering the full scope of pediatric dental knowledge — from growth and development to behavior management and clinical procedures.

The platform features an AI Smart Tutor that gives you detailed explanations and personalized feedback so you're not just memorizing answers, but truly understanding the material.

Who is PedBoards QE for?

PedBoards QE is designed for:

  • Pediatric dentistry residents preparing for Part 1 or Part 2 board exams
  • General dentists pursuing board certification or recertification in pediatric dentistry
  • Students and clinicians who want to strengthen their pediatric dental knowledge

Whether you're a few months out from your exam or just starting to build a study habit, PedBoards QE adapts to where you are.

How do I sign up?

Getting started is quick:

  1. Visit pedsdentqe.com
  2. Click Sign Up or Get Started
  3. Create your account with your email address and a password
  4. Complete checkout ($35/month via Stripe)
  5. You'll have immediate access to the full question bank
💡 Access is granted instantly after payment. No waiting period or manual approval required.
Do UT Health residents get free access?

Yes! UT Health San Antonio pediatric dentistry residents receive complimentary access to PedBoards QE as part of their program.

If you're a UT Health resident and haven't received your access credentials, please reach out to your program director or contact us directly at support@pedsdentqe.com with your institutional email address and we'll get you set up.

Billing & Refunds

How much does it cost?

PedBoards QE is $35/month, billed monthly. There are no setup fees, no contracts, and no hidden charges.

Your subscription renews automatically on the same calendar day each month. You can cancel at any time before your next renewal date to avoid being charged for the following month.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime through the Stripe customer portal — no need to contact us:

  1. Visit the Billing Portal
  2. Log in with the email address used at checkout
  3. Select Cancel Plan

After canceling, you retain access through the end of your current paid period. Your subscription will not renew after that date.

ℹ️ Need help canceling? Email us at support@pedsdentqe.com and we'll handle it for you promptly.
What is your refund policy?

We offer a 24-hour refund window from the time of your initial purchase. If you're unsatisfied for any reason within the first 24 hours, contact us at support@pedsdentqe.com and we will issue a full refund.

⚠️ After 24 hours, all sales are final. No refunds will be issued for charges beyond the initial 24-hour window, including partial-month usage or subscription renewals.

We recommend exploring the platform fully within your first day to make sure it's the right fit for your study needs.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards through Stripe, including:

  • Visa
  • Mastercard
  • American Express
  • Discover

All transactions are processed securely by Stripe. We never store your full card details on our servers.

Using the Platform

How does the AI Smart Tutor work?

The AI Smart Tutor is built directly into the question review experience. After you answer a question — whether correct or incorrect — you can ask the AI follow-up questions about the topic, request a deeper explanation, or ask it to relate the concept to a clinical scenario.

It's designed to behave like having a knowledgeable co-resident on call: contextual, responsive, and focused on the specific content you're studying. It can:

  • Explain why the correct answer is right (and why distractors are wrong)
  • Summarize related concepts for deeper understanding
  • Help you connect the dots between similar topics
✨ The more you engage with it, the more targeted and useful its explanations become.
How many questions are there?

PedBoards QE contains a continuously growing bank of high-yield questions spanning the full scope of the pediatric dentistry boards exam. Questions are organized by topic and updated regularly to reflect current guidelines and exam formats.

New questions are added on an ongoing basis, so your question bank keeps growing during your subscription — you're never working from a static set.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — PedBoards QE is fully mobile-responsive and works on any modern smartphone or tablet browser. No app download required.

Just navigate to pedsdentqe.com, log in, and you're ready to study from anywhere — on rounds, between cases, or on the go.

My question seems incorrect — what should I do?

We take content accuracy seriously. If you believe a question, answer choice, or explanation contains an error, please flag it so we can review it.

Send us an email at support@pedsdentqe.com with:

  • The question text (or question number if shown)
  • What you believe is incorrect
  • A reference or source if you have one (guideline, textbook, etc.)

Our team reviews all flagged questions and will update the platform if a correction is warranted. We appreciate your help keeping the content sharp.

Account & Technical

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

Resetting your password is easy:

  1. Go to the login page
  2. Click Forgot Password?
  3. Enter the email address associated with your account
  4. Check your inbox for a reset link (also check your spam/junk folder)
  5. Click the link and set a new password
ℹ️ Password reset links expire after 60 minutes. If yours has expired, simply request a new one from the login page.
I paid but can't access the platform — what do I do?

This is uncommon but can happen due to a few reasons. Try these steps first:

  1. Hard refresh the page (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac / Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows)
  2. Log out and back in to your account
  3. Make sure you're logged in with the same email address used during checkout
  4. Check your email for a confirmation or receipt from Stripe to confirm the charge went through

If you're still unable to access the platform after trying the above, email us at support@pedsdentqe.com with your name and the email used at checkout. We'll manually verify and restore your access within a few hours.

⚠️ Please include your Stripe receipt or transaction ID if available — it helps us locate your account faster.
